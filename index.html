Cue words you can say while talking
  • “New scene” / “new chapter” / “new section”: start a new unit
  • “Scratch that”: drops the sentence you just said
  • “Note to self …”: a margin note, never prose
  • “Verbatim … end verbatim”: those words go in exactly, untouched
  • “Character note”, “setting note”, “idea note”, “source note”: bible material
  • “New paragraph”: a paragraph break
